How Watch Link uses your data

Watch Link is a personal app that connects your Noise watch to your phone. It has no servers of its own and doesn't sell or share your data.

Health data

  • Steps, distance, calories, heart rate, sleep and battery are read from your watch over Bluetooth and stored on your phone.
  • If you connect Health Connect, the app writes that data there so apps you choose (like Google Fit) can show it. It only writes; it doesn't read other apps' health data.

Google account

  • If you sign in with Google, the app uses your name and photo to personalise the screen.
  • Backups go to a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive, which only Watch Link can see. It can't see your other files.

Ask Claude

  • When you ask a question, a summary of your watch data is sent with it to Anthropic's Claude API using your own API key.

Weather and music

  • Your city or approximate location is sent to Open-Meteo to get the forecast.
  • Notification access, if you allow it, is used only to see the song that's playing.

Removing your data

  • Uninstalling the app deletes everything on the phone. Remove the Drive backup in Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps, and Health Connect data in Settings → Health Connect.
