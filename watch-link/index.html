Watch not in the list?
  1. Free the watch: in phone Settings → Bluetooth, tap ⚙️ next to the watch → Disconnect (not Forget), then force-stop NoiseFit. A watch that's connected to the phone stays hidden from Chrome.
  2. Turn on Bluetooth and Location on your phone.
  3. Tap Connect to watch and pick your watch from the list.

Connect to watch lists only Noise and MOYOUNG-based watches. If the list is empty, the watch is still connected to the phone or NoiseFit. Show all devices (under More → Advanced) lists everything nearby.

Reconnect saved watch skips the scan and connects straight to a watch you've picked before, so it can work even while the watch is connected to your phone or NoiseFit. Pick the watch once with Connect to watch first.

Watch face

Experimental: send a face file to the watch

Sends a MoYoung-format face file to the watch over Bluetooth, the same way the dawfu tool does. Connect to the watch first, keep it charged above 50%, and keep this page open until it finishes (about 1–3 minutes). Try Tiny test first: it's 16 chunks instead of 1,300, so it shows within seconds whether the watch accepts uploads at all. If the watch rejects the face or it looks wrong, pick another face in NoiseFit.
